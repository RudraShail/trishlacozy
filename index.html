FOUNDER'S VISION

"A Blanket Should Feel Like Coming Home"

TRISHLACOZY was born out of a simple observation: most blankets either sacrifice warmth for aesthetics, or quickly lose their softness after a few washes.

We set out on a dedicated mission to re-engineer plush textiles. By marrying high-grade synthetic mink fibers with traditional weaving techniques, we created blankets that remain luxuriously soft, dense, and resilient year after year.

"True luxury is not defined by excess, but by the quiet comfort it brings to your daily moments. Every TRISHLACOZY piece is created with love, durability, and family warmth in mind."
Trishla Sharma
Founder & Head of Product Design Trishla Cozy
MILESTONES & EVOLUTION

The TRISHLACOZY Journey

From humble handlooms to a state-of-the-art textile brand trusted by luxury homes, boutique hotels, and regional distributors.

2001 — The Spark
Artisanal Textile Research

Started as a passion project evaluating cotton and synthetic fleece weaving techniques across historic textile clusters.

2008 — Innovation
Mink Fleece Manufacturing

Established our first specialized micro-fleece spinning unit to produce non-shedding, heavy GSM plush blankets.

2015 — Expansion
5-Star Hotel Supply Launch

Partnered with luxury hospitality chains and boutique resorts to engineer ultra-durable, washable hotel bedding collections.

2020 — Trust
1 Million Blankets Delivered

Crossed a key benchmark of shipping over 1 million cozy blankets across 100+ cities with 99.4% customer satisfaction.

2024+ — The Future
Eco-Crafted Weaves

Introducing recycled micro-fibers and zero-carbon ethical manufacturing to ensure comfort beyond generations.
